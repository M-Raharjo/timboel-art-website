# Sculptures Inspired by the Wild

![](/uploads/wild-majesty-hero.jpeg)

We present **Wild Majesty**, a collection inspired by the beauty of wildlife free, captivating, yet full of challenges. Beyond the wilderness lies a unique beauty in the strength, tranquility, and character of every creature that inhabits it. It is this contrast that we bring to life through our sculptural works.

## From Wildlife to Sculpture

The wilderness and its wildlife offer a diverse range of forms and characters, each with its own distinctive appeal. To us, every animal carries an expression and symbolism that can be translated into a work of art.

The horse with its agile movements, the lion with its courage, the deer with its grace and serenity, and the orangutan with its agility and distinctive character.

With a craftsmanship heritage dating back to 1997, we translate these characteristics into the **Wild Majesty** collection through carefully designed forms and details that bring each sculpture to life and make it instantly recognizable.

To us, detail is more than just part of the creation process. It is an essential element that gives each work of art its character and life.

## Bringing Wild Majesty Into a Space

**Wild Majesty** is designed to make a bold statement in any space, whether indoors or outdoors.

When placed in open spaces, however, these sculptures seem to rediscover the very environment that inspired them. Amid gardens, resorts, or private residences, each sculpture evokes the spirit of the wilderness while serving as a striking focal point.
